## Description
RugCheck scans memecoins for the classic warning signs of a rug pull — in seconds.

Paste a token contract address from Solana, Ethereum, Base or BNB Chain and RugCheck pulls live data from trusted public sources (RugCheck.xyz, DexScreener, GeckoTerminal, GoPlus Security and honeypot.is) to give you:

• A 0–100 risk score and a simple verdict: Likely Legit, Caution, High Risk or Likely Scam
• Red flags explained in plain English — active mint or freeze authority, unlocked liquidity, whale concentration, honeypot sell taxes, hidden owner powers and more
• Good signs, so you can see what checks passed
• Market telemetry: liquidity, 24h volume and token age
• Recent scan history saved privately on your device

No account. No wallet connection. No tracking. RugCheck never asks for your keys or seed phrase.

Disclaimer: RugCheck is an automated heuristic tool for information only and is not financial advice. Always do your own research.
