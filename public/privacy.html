PRIVACY POLICY

RugCheck · Effective October 24, 2026

RugCheck ("the app") is a free tool that estimates the rug-pull risk of crypto tokens. This policy explains what happens to information when you use the iOS app or the website at rugcheck-ud3r.onrender.com.

Information we collect

We do not ask for, collect, or store any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no tracking.

Token addresses you scan

When you scan, the token contract address you enter is sent to our server, which looks it up using third-party public APIs: RugCheck (rugcheck.xyz), DexScreener, GeckoTerminal, GoPlus Security, and honeypot.is. Those services receive the token address only, never information about you. Our server keeps short-lived in-memory caches of market data (a few minutes) and does not log or store your scans.

Local history

Your recent scans (token address, name, score, verdict, time) are stored only on your device so you can revisit them. You can delete them any time with "Clear history" or by deleting the app.

Standard network data

Like any internet service, our hosting provider (Render) may process your IP address in routine server logs for security and operations. We do not use it to identify you.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and its effective date.
